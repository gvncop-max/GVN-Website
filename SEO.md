## Search Console (Valentine, about 10 minutes)
1. Go to search.google.com/search-console, sign in with the business Google account.
2. Add property, choose **Domain**, type `gvnestateinvest.com`.
3. Google shows a TXT record (`google-site-verification=...`). In IONOS DNS add a
   **TXT** record, host `@`, with that value. Do not edit the existing SPF TXT.
4. Back in Search Console press Verify (can take up to an hour).
5. Then Sitemaps: submit `https://www.gvnestateinvest.com/sitemap.xml`.

## The list of links under the result (sitelinks)
Google chooses whether to show them; nobody can switch them on. What helps,
and is already in place: a clear menu, distinct page titles, a sitemap, and
internal links. They tend to appear once the site gets steady searches for
the brand name ("GVN Estate Invest").

## Backlink plan (honest, low cost, no paid link schemes)
Links from relevant, real UK sites. In order of effort:
1. **Profiles that link back** (one afternoon): Google Business Profile (most
   important for local search), Bing Places, Companies House-linked
   directories, Yell, Thomson Local, FreeIndex, Cylex, the ICO public register
   entry, and every social profile (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok,
   YouTube, X) with the website in the bio. Same name, address and phone on all.
2. **Local Staffordshire** (ongoing): Stoke-on-Trent and Staffordshire business
   directories, Staffordshire Chambers of Commerce, local networking event
   pages (most list attendees or speakers), local news (a story about a
   refurbishment bringing an empty home back into use).
3. **Property industry**: property investor forums and communities with a
   profile link (property118, Property Tribes), podcast guest spots, and
   guest articles for UK property blogs, reusing The Provenance Pulse research.
4. **Content worth linking to**: the Provenance Pulse posts on Renters' Rights,
   MTD and the 60-day CGT clock are the kind other sites cite. Keep them
   current and accurate; one solid guide earns more links than ten short posts.
5. **Partners**: solicitors, brokers, letting agents and tradespeople in the
   power team, plus the umbrella sourcing partner: a mutual "partners" mention
   on each other's sites.
Avoid: bought links, link exchanges at scale, private blog networks. Google
penalises them and they are hard to undo.
