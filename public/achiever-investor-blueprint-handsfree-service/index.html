The GVN Blueprint

The Achiever Investor journey

The documented 5-step client journey for Achiever Investor Package hands-free investors.

  1. Hands-Free Property Sourcing

    This is where we shine. Using our off-market networks and data-driven analysis of Staffordshire regeneration hotspots, we curate off-market deals based on your goals and yield analysis, presenting you with 3–5 exclusive deals that match your criteria (budget, yield, location). We handle all negotiations.

  2. Stress-Free Refurbishment to Legal Standards

    Our project manager oversees the entire refurb, using our network of trusted contractors, to standards that exceed the Decent Homes Standard and Awaab’s Law. The work should not only look great but also future-proof your investment against regulatory changes.

  3. Vetted Lettings & Tenancy Management

    Our vetted lettings team handles tenant sourcing and full reference checks, creates a legally watertight tenancy agreement, and professionally markets the property. They handle the entire move-in process.

  4. The Arrival of Passive Income

    Full property management: the lettings agent collects the rent, manages all tenant queries, and conducts periodic property inspections. You receive a detailed statement on the same date each month. This is the “hands-free, stress-free” reality.

  5. Strategic Refinance (The Optional Growth Lever) Optional

    After 6–12 months, once the property has been refurbished and tenanted, we assess its new market value. We then guide you through refinancing to pull out your initial capital (or a portion of it) to reinvest into your next property, recycling your capital.
