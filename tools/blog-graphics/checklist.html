Register your rental property · West Midlands, 15 Dec 2026 to 14 Mar 2027

Have these ready before you log in

What the landlord database asks for, from the GOV.UK guidance and the draft regulations.

About you and the property

  • A GOV.UK One Login account
  • Your name, date of birth, address, phone and email
  • Each property's address, type and number of bedrooms
  • How you own it, and contacts for any freeholder, superior landlord or managing agent
  • Any HMO, additional or selective licence

About the tenancy (within 28 days)

  • Gas safety record
  • Electrical report (EICR or EIC)
  • EPC, or a valid exemption
  • Rent, bills included, furnished or not
  • Number of occupants and households
